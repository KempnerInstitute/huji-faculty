About the workshop

The KELSC NeuroAI Workshop brings together researchers from the Kempner Institute at Harvard University and the Edmond & Lily Safra Center for Brain Sciences at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem for three days of talks, posters, and discussion at the intersection of neuroscience and artificial intelligence.

The program pairs invited talks with contributed posters and open discussion sessions, with the aim of seeding new collaborations and strengthening existing ties between the two institutes.
